Profit Margin vs. Markup: Key Differences

Although margin and markup use the same gross profit dollar figure, they divide by different denominators—which is why a 50% markup equals a 33.3% gross margin.

Gross Profit = Selling Price − Cost
Gross Margin (%) = (Gross Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100
Markup (%) = (Gross Profit ÷ Cost) × 100

For sales teams and deal desks, discounting directly erodes gross profit dollar-for-dollar because delivery cost stays fixed. A 10% discount on a 40% margin deal actually cuts net profit by 25%.
