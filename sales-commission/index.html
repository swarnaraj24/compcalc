How Sales Commission Is Calculated

A sales commission plan rewards sales representatives based on closed revenue or gross profit. Depending on your compensation agreement, commissions typically follow one of three structures:

  • Flat-Rate Commission: You earn a fixed percentage on every dollar sold, regardless of volume.
  • Quota Accelerator Commission: You earn a standard base commission rate up to 100% of your quota, and a higher accelerated rate on all revenue closed above quota.
  • Progressive Tiered Commission: Revenue is divided into brackets (similar to marginal tax brackets), with each bracket paying a progressively higher commission percentage.

Core Formulas

Flat Commission = Total Sales × (Commission Rate ÷ 100)
Quota Attainment (%) = (Total Sales ÷ Quota Target) × 100
Accelerator Payout = (Quota × Base Rate) + ((Total Sales − Quota) × Accelerator Rate)

Worked Example

Suppose an Account Executive has a monthly quota of $100,000, a base commission rate of 8%, and an accelerator rate of 12% above quota. If they close $120,000 in a month:

  • First $100,000 × 8% = $8,000
  • Remaining $20,000 above quota × 12% = $2,400
  • Total Earned Commission = $10,400 (an effective blended rate of 8.67%).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a recoverable vs. non-recoverable draw?
During onboarding, companies often provide a guaranteed monthly draw while a rep builds pipeline. If your draw is $3,000 and you only earn $1,800 in commission, a recoverable draw means you still receive $3,000 this month, but the $1,200 shortfall is deducted from future commission checks. A non-recoverable draw acts as a guaranteed minimum bonus with no clawback.
