About CompCalc

Transparent, browser-based financial calculators built for sales professionals, Revenue Operations teams, founders, and freelancers.

Our Purpose

Sales compensation plans and pay structures often involve multi-bracket tiers, quota attainment accelerators, recoverable draws, and margin-versus-markup conversions that are tedious to model manually. CompCalc provides clear, single-purpose calculators with transparent step-by-step formulas and worked examples.

Calculation Methodology & Privacy

Every calculator on CompCalc executes 100% client-side inside your web browser using standard financial and incentive-compensation formulas. We do not collect, transmit, or store the salary, quota, or deal figures you enter into any calculator.

Editorial Standards

Each tool is accompanied by plain-language explanations, formula breakdowns, and practical examples so users can verify how every output is derived. If you spot a calculation edge case or want to request a new compensation model, reach out via our Contact page.
