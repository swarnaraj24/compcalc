How to Convert Hourly Wage to Annual Salary

To find your base annual salary before taxes, multiply your hourly rate by the number of hours you work each week, and then multiply by the number of paid weeks in a year.

Annual Base Salary = Hourly Rate × Hours per Week × Weeks per Year
Total Annual Pay = Annual Base Salary + (Monthly Commission × 12)

For a standard 40-hour workweek across 52 weeks (2,080 hours total), a quick mental shortcut is to multiply your hourly wage by 2,000 and add 4%—for example, $30/hour × 2,080 = $62,400 per year.
