Understanding OTE and Quota-to-OTE Ratios

On-Target Earnings (OTE) represents your full expected annual cash compensation when hitting 100% of your assigned sales target. Unlike a guaranteed salary, OTE combines fixed base pay with at-risk variable commission.

OTE = Annual Base Salary + On-Target Variable Commission
Implied Commission Rate (%) = (Target Variable ÷ Annual Quota) × 100
Quota-to-OTE Ratio = Annual Quota ÷ OTE

In B2B SaaS, a healthy Quota-to-OTE ratio typically ranges between 4x and 6x. If a role offers $150,000 OTE with a $750,000 annual quota (5x ratio), the quota is generally aligned with industry benchmarks. Ratios above 8x–10x often indicate an aggressive or difficult-to-attain target unless average deal sizes are large.
